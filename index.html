What is phone screen resolution?

Screen resolution is the number of tiny dots — pixels — your display uses to draw everything you see. It is written as width × height, for example 1080 × 2400. More pixels usually mean a sharper picture, but the number alone does not tell the whole story: pixel density and screen size matter just as much.

📱 Physical pixels

The real hardware resolution of your screen — the actual number of pixels built into the panel. This is the number phone makers advertise.

🖥️ Viewport (CSS pixels)

The size websites see. Because of the device pixel ratio, the viewport is smaller than the physical resolution — that is why pages look the same size on very different phones.

🔍 Pixel ratio (DPR)

How many physical pixels make up one CSS pixel. A DPR of 3 means every CSS pixel is drawn with 3 × 3 real pixels, which is why text looks so crisp on modern phones.

✨ PPI (pixels per inch)

Pixel density — how tightly the pixels are packed. Above roughly 300 PPI, the human eye can no longer tell individual pixels apart at normal viewing distance.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find my phone's screen resolution?

Open this page on your phone — the tool shows your resolution instantly at the top. No app, no settings, no download. You can also find it in your phone's Settings under About phone, but the exact menu name differs by brand.

What is the difference between screen resolution and viewport size?

Screen resolution is the physical number of pixels on your display. Viewport size is the size in CSS pixels that websites use to lay out pages. Because of the device pixel ratio, the viewport is smaller — for example, a phone with a 1179 × 2556 screen may report a viewport of 393 × 852 CSS pixels.

What is a good screen resolution for a phone?

For most people, Full HD (1080p) is plenty sharp on a phone-sized screen. Quad HD (1440p) looks slightly crisper on large phones, mainly for VR and very close viewing. Above 1080p the difference is hard to notice in everyday use.

What is PPI and why does it matter?

PPI means pixels per inch — how densely pixels are packed. Higher PPI means sharper text and images. Around 300 PPI is the point where individual pixels become invisible to the eye at normal distance; most modern phones are well above 400 PPI.

Does a higher resolution drain the battery faster?

Yes, a little. More pixels mean the processor and graphics chip work harder, especially in games and video. Some phones let you lower the resolution in Settings to save battery.

Can I change my phone's screen resolution?

On many Android phones you can: look in Settings under Display for "Screen resolution" and pick HD+, FHD+ or QHD+. On iPhones the resolution is fixed and cannot be changed.

Why does my resolution look different in different apps?

Some apps and games render at a lower resolution to run faster, and some phones switch resolution automatically to save battery. This tool reads the real hardware resolution, so it is the number to trust.

What is device pixel ratio (DPR)?

DPR tells you how many physical pixels are used for each CSS pixel. A DPR of 2 means 2 × 2 real pixels per CSS pixel; a DPR of 3 means 3 × 3. Higher DPR gives sharper text and images.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely free. The resolution check, the details and the PPI calculator are all free, with no limits and no account.

Do I need to install anything?

No. This is a web tool — everything runs in your browser on any phone, tablet or computer. Nothing is installed, uploaded or stored.

Tips for a sharper screen

🔆 Match your content

Watch videos in the highest quality your connection allows — a 1080p screen cannot show more detail than its pixels, but a low-quality video wastes the ones it has.

🔋 Save battery on QHD phones

If your phone offers a resolution setting, dropping from QHD to FHD+ saves battery with almost no visible difference.

🧹 Keep the glass clean

Fingerprints and smudges blur the picture more than a few missing pixels ever will. A quick wipe makes any screen look sharper.

📏 PPI beats raw pixels

When comparing phones, look at PPI and screen size together — a smaller screen with fewer pixels can look sharper than a big one with more.

🎮 Games and resolution

Many games let you pick a render resolution. Lowering it makes games run smoother; raising it makes them look crisper.

🌙 Check at full brightness

Resolution is only half the picture — brightness, color and contrast decide how good the screen actually looks. Test those too.
