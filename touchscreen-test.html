    What is a touchscreen test?

    A touchscreen test checks whether every part of your display responds to touch. It finds dead spots — small areas that ignore your finger — checks how many fingers the screen can track at once, and shows whether touches lag or jump around.

    🎯 Dead spots

    A dead spot is an area of the screen that does not respond to taps. The tap grid above covers the whole screen, so a dead spot shows up as a square that never turns green.

    ✌️ Multi-touch

    Modern phones track several fingers at once — for pinch to zoom, games and gestures. The multi-touch pad shows live how many touch points your screen sees.

    ✏️ Draw test

    Drawing a line across the screen is the fastest way to spot a problem. A smooth line means the screen is fine; a broken or wobbly line points to a dead or weak area.

    ⚡ Instant result

    No app, no download, no waiting. The tests run the moment the page loads and give you an answer in under a minute.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I test my phone touchscreen?

    Open this page on your phone and tap "Start Touch Test". The screen goes fullscreen and 14 tests run one after another — swipe grids, tap targets, multi-touch, edges, corners, ghost touch, latency, then a white screen and solid colors. Mark each test as you go and read your results at the end. The whole check takes a few minutes and needs no app.

    What is a dead spot on a touchscreen?

    A dead spot is a small area of the screen that does not respond to touch at all. It is usually caused by damage to the digitizer — the layer under the glass that senses your finger. In the tap grid above, a dead spot shows up as a square that never turns green.

    How many fingers can my phone track at once?

    Most modern phones track 5 to 10 touch points at the same time. Put as many fingers as you can on the multi-touch pad above and watch the counter — that number is your phone's limit.

    Why does part of my screen not respond to touch?

    The most common causes are a cracked screen, a damaged digitizer, a thick or faulty screen protector, or dirt and moisture on the glass. If the same spot fails in every test on this page, it is almost certainly a hardware fault.

    What are ghost touches?

    Ghost touches are taps and swipes the screen makes on its own, with no finger touching it. They are often caused by a faulty charger, moisture, a damaged screen, or a case pressing on the edges. Try removing the case and protector, cleaning the screen, and restarting the phone.

    Can a screen protector cause touch problems?

    Yes. A thick, dirty, bubbled or badly fitted screen protector can block touches, especially near the edges. If problems started after fitting a protector, try removing it and testing the screen again — many phones also have a "touch sensitivity" setting for use with protectors.

    My touchscreen works only sometimes — why?

    Intermittent touch is often caused by moisture, a loose connection inside the phone, a faulty charger sending interference, or a software bug. If it happens with a particular charger, stop using it. If it happens everywhere, update your software and, if it continues, have the phone checked.

    Does this test work on a laptop or desktop?

    Yes. Every test works with a mouse: click the squares, click and hold on the multi-touch pad, and drag to draw. The multi-touch counter will show one point for a mouse — that is normal, since a mouse is a single pointer.

    Tips to fix touchscreen issues

    🔄 Restart your phone

    A restart clears temporary glitches and is the first thing to try when touch acts up. It fixes more problems than most people expect.

    🧹 Clean the screen

    Dirt, grease and moisture confuse the touch sensor. Wipe the screen with a soft, slightly damp cloth and dry it before testing again.

    🛡️ Check the protector and case

    Remove the screen protector and case, then test again. If the problem goes away, the protector or case was pressing on the screen.

    🔌 Try another charger

    Cheap or damaged chargers can cause ghost touches and lag while charging. Unplug and test — if the problem stops, replace the charger.

    ⬆️ Update your software

    Touch bugs are often fixed in system updates. Check for updates and install them, then run the test again.

    🔧 Last resort: get it checked

    If the same spot fails every test even after a restart and a clean, the digitizer is probably damaged. A repair shop can test it and replace the screen.
